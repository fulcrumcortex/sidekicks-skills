# Arrears: staged contact

Stages are counted in days since the rent due date, from the ledger. The statutory notice stage, the minimum arrears period before a notice, and the form of notice are set by each state's residential tenancy law. Confirm them before drafting any formal notice. The PM approves every message individually from stage 3 on.

## Before any contact

- [ ] Check receipting: payments in transit, bank processing days, a payment receipted to the wrong ledger
- [ ] Check for an agreed payment plan and whether this payment is within it
- [ ] Check for a Centrepay or similar scheduled deduction that runs on a different cycle
- [ ] Check notes for hardship, family violence, illness or a bereavement. If present, stop and hand to the PM; do not send stage messages.
- [ ] Check that the contact details are current and that the tenant has not asked to be contacted another way

## Stages

| Stage | Days in arrears | Channel | Tone and content | Owner informed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 to 3 | SMS | Friendly: "our records show rent due on [date] has not arrived; if you have paid, ignore this". | No |
| 2 | 4 to 7 | Phone call, then email | Ask what has happened and when payment will be made. Offer a payment arrangement within the agency's policy. Record the promise. | No, unless the owner asked to be told at day 1 |
| 3 | 8 to 13 | Email and letter | Formal reminder: amount, due date, ledger summary, what happens next. PM approves. | Yes, with the plan |
| 4 | State minimum reached (often around 14 days; check) | Statutory notice in the state's form | The PM prepares and serves the notice; the skill only drafts the covering message and diary dates. | Yes, with owner instructions requested |
| 5 | After the notice period | Tribunal application or agreed plan | PM and owner decision. The skill prepares the ledger and contact history. | Yes |

Broken payment promise: move to the next stage on the day after the promised date, not from the start again.

## Templates

**Stage 1 SMS**

> Hi [first name], [agency] property management here. Our records show rent of $[amount] due on [date] for [address] has not been received yet. If you have already paid, please ignore this. Otherwise reply or call [PM name] on [number]. Thanks.

**Stage 2 email after a call**

> Hi [first name], thanks for speaking with me today. As discussed, rent of $[amount] for [address] is [n] days overdue. You said you will pay $[amount] on [date]. If anything changes, please let me know before that date. [PM name], [agency]

**Stage 3 formal reminder (PM approves)**

> Dear [name], rent for [address] is now [n] days in arrears. The amount owing at [date] is $[amount] (ledger attached). Please pay this amount by [date] or contact me to discuss. If the arrears are not paid or an arrangement agreed, we may need to take further steps under the residential tenancy laws of [state]. [PM name], [agency]

## Never

- Threaten action the PM has not decided on, or quote legislation in an SMS.
- Contact the tenant's employer, family or references about arrears.
- Send arrears messages outside reasonable hours (default 8am to 8pm on weekdays, 9am to 5pm Saturday, none on Sunday or public holidays).
- Tell the owner more about the tenant's circumstances than the owner needs to decide.

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.
