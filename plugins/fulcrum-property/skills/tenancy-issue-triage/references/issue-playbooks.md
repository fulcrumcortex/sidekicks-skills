# Issue playbooks: strata, utilities, insurance, owner approvals

Each playbook gives the owner of the item, the default deadline and the next document needed. Replace the defaults with the agency's own service standards when the PM supplies them.

## Strata (owners corporation, body corporate)

| Situation | Owner | Deadline | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common property defect reported by tenant (roof, shared pipes, lift, foyer) | PM | Same day for urgent, 1 business day otherwise | Report to the strata manager in writing with photos; tell the tenant it is a strata matter and who is handling it |
| Leak where the source (lot or common property) is unknown | PM | Same day | Notify strata manager and arrange a leak detection if strata will not attend; record who pays as "to be determined" |
| By-law breach notice received (noise, parking, pets) | PM | Before the date in the notice | Forward to the tenant with a plain explanation; inform the owner |
| Levy notice or special levy | Owner (PM forwards) | Due date on the notice | Forward to owner or pay from rent if the management agreement says so |
| AGM or meeting notice | Owner | Proxy deadline in the notice | Forward to owner with the agenda items that affect the lot |

## Utilities

| Situation | Owner | Deadline | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water usage bill received | PM | Within the state's timeframe for passing on usage charges (check) | Calculate the tenant's share from meter readings at the start and end of the tenancy and the lease terms; charge only usage the lease and state rules allow |
| Supply cut or disconnection notice | PM | Same day | Confirm whose account it is; if it is the owner's, contact the owner immediately |
| Tenant asks who pays for a service | PM | 1 business day | Check the lease and whether the property is separately metered; answer from the lease, not memory |

## Insurance

| Situation | Owner | Deadline | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Storm, water, fire or impact damage | PM informs owner; owner or PM lodges claim per the agreement | Same day notice to owner; insurer's notification window | Photos, date and time, tenant statement, make-safe invoice. Do not admit liability or promise cover. |
| Malicious damage or theft by a tenant | PM | Same day | Police event number if reported; owner's landlord insurer notified; ledger and condition reports attached |
| Loss of rent claim | Owner with PM support | Insurer's window | Ledger, arrears contact history, notices served |
| Tenant asks if their belongings are covered | PM | 1 business day | Tell the tenant the owner's policy generally does not cover tenants' contents and suggest they check their own policy. Do not give insurance advice. |

## Owner approvals

| Situation | Owner | Deadline | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Routine repair over the approval limit | Owner | Chase at 2 business days, call at 4 | Send issue, photos, quotes, recommendation and the consequence of waiting |
| Owner has not replied and the repair is now urgent | PM | Same day | Act within the emergency authority in the management agreement; notify the owner in writing |
| Rent increase, lease renewal or new lease terms | Owner | Before the state's notice deadline | Send market evidence and the recommendation; diary the notice date |
| Owner wants something the law may not allow (for example entry without notice) | PM | Before acting | Explain the constraint, propose a lawful alternative, record the instruction |

## Draft standards

- Tenant replies: what was received, what happens next, by when, and who to contact.
- Owner requests: one decision per email, with a recommended option and a reply-by date.
- Work orders: job number, address, issue, access, tenant contact (with consent), approval limit, invoice-to details.
