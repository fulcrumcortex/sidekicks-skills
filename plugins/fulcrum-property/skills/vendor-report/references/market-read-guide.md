# Market read and recommendations guide

Use these rules to turn the week's numbers into an honest read. They are rules of thumb for a typical residential campaign; adjust when the agent gives local context, and say that you did.

## Reading the numbers

| Pattern | Likely meaning | Say it like this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiries and groups both down more than 30% week on week after week 2 | Normal campaign decay; the first-look audience has been through | "Most active buyers have now seen the property; the next buyers will come from new listings alerts and return visits." |
| Groups steady, zero contract requests by week 3 | Interest without commitment; usually price or a specific objection | "Buyers are looking but not taking the next step. Feedback points to [theme]." |
| Two or more second inspections or contract requests | Genuine buyer pool forming | "We have [n] buyers doing their due diligence." |
| Price feedback mostly "below guide" from 50% or more of groups who commented | Guide is above what this buyer pool will pay now | "Most buyers who commented see the guide as above their range. This is a pattern, not one opinion." |
| One objection raised by 40% or more of groups | A fixable or disclosable issue is costing interest | "[Theme] came up with [x of y] groups." |
| Competing listing launched nearby at a lower guide | Buyers will compare directly | "A comparable home at [address] launched at [guide]; buyers will compare the two." |
| Fewer than 5 groups in total for the week | Too small a sample for themes | Report themes as "early comments", not patterns. |

## Honesty rules

- Lead with facts, then interpretation, then the recommendation.
- Say the uncomfortable thing once, clearly, and with the evidence. Do not soften it into nothing or repeat it.
- Never blame the vendor, the property or the buyers. Describe the gap between expectation and evidence.
- Do not predict a sale price or describe the property's value.
- If the sample is small or sources are missing, say so before drawing conclusions.

## Recommendation menu

Choose two to four. Each needs a reason from the evidence and an owner.

| Trigger | Recommendation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guide repeatedly seen as high, week 3 or later | Discuss adjusting the price guide (vendor decides) | Vendor and agent |
| Objection is fixable cheaply | Obtain a tradesperson quote to share with buyers, or fix before next open | Vendor |
| Objection is about information (strata, rates, reports) | Prepare an information sheet or pre-purchase reports for buyers | Agent |
| Low weekday availability in feedback | Add a twilight or weekday private inspection slot | Agent |
| Online interest high, inspections low | Review photography, floor plan or headline | Agent |
| Buyer pool forming | Set a clear offers process and timeline, or confirm auction registration steps | Agent |
| Competing listing launched | Prepare a side-by-side for buyers who mention it | Agent |
| Interest strong and steady | Hold the course; no change recommended | Agent |
