# Message templates

Fill every bracket from the register, the agent's notes or the listing. If a bracket cannot be filled from those sources, cut the sentence rather than guess. Keep SMS under 300 characters where possible. Every message names the agent and agency.

## Hot

**SMS (within 1 hour)**

> Hi [first name], [agent first name] from [agency] here. Thanks for coming through [street address] today. You asked about [their question]. I can send the contract of sale and vendor documents now and talk you through [offers or auction] process. Is a quick call at [time options] OK? Reply STOP to opt out.

**Email**

Subject: [Street address]: the documents you asked for

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for inspecting [street address] today. As promised:
>
> - Contract of sale and vendor disclosure documents: [attached by the agent / available on request]
> - [Answer to the specific question they asked]
> - Next inspections: [day, date, time]
> - [Auction date and registration details / How offers are being handled, as the agent has confirmed]
>
> I will call you [day/time agreed]. If something else would help you decide, reply here.
>
> [Agent name], [agency], [mobile]
> [Unsubscribe line]

## Warm

**SMS or email**

> Hi [first name], thanks for coming through [street address] today. You asked about [question]; [answer, or "I am checking that and will come back to you by [day]"]. Next inspection is [day, time]. [Agent first name], [agency]. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Cold

**Email**

Subject: Thanks for inspecting [street address]

> Hi [first name], thanks for coming through today. I know it might not be quite right for you. If you tell me what you are looking for, I will let you know when something closer comes up [only include if they consented to similar listings]. [Agent name], [agency]. [Unsubscribe line]

## Just looking

> Hi [first name], thanks for stopping by [street address] today. [Agent name], [agency].

Add an appraisal offer only if the attendee asked for one, or has consented to marketing and owns nearby.

## Contract or disclosure request acknowledgement

> Hi [first name], [agent first name] here. I will send the contract of sale and vendor disclosure documents for [street address] to [email] [today/by time]. Questions on the contract are best answered by your solicitor or conveyancer.

Use the state's own document name only when the agent supplies it.

## What never goes in a follow-up

- A price figure other than the published guide.
- Another buyer's offer, interest level or feedback ("there's strong interest" only if the agent confirms it is true and wants it said).
- Claims about the property not in the listing copy or the agent's notes (zoning, school catchment, development approvals).
- Pressure language with a false deadline.
