# Booking rules

Use the venue's own numbers when you have them. The defaults below are typical for a mid-size à la carte restaurant and are marked as defaults in every answer that relies on them.

## Capacity has three limits

1. **Seats**: covers in the room at the same time. A 60-seat room is full at 60 covers, but is usually full in practice at 85 to 90% because tables of four seat couples.
2. **Kitchen pacing**: covers arriving in each 15-minute slot. A common limit is 12 to 20 covers per 15 minutes for a single kitchen line. Over-pacing causes slow tickets for everyone in the room.
3. **Tables**: a party needs a real table or an approved combination. A party of 10 needs a combination that may block 12 to 14 seats.

A request fits only when all three hold.

## Default turn times

| Party size | Table held | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 2 | 90 minutes | 75 at lunch |
| 3 to 4 | 105 minutes | |
| 5 to 6 | 120 minutes | |
| 7 or more | 150 minutes | Set menu often required |

Tell guests the table time only if the venue states it in its policy, and phrase it as "we have the table for you until [time]".

## Service periods

- Accept arrivals only from opening to last seating. A booking at 21:15 for a 21:00 last seating is a "does not fit" unless the manager approves.
- Watch the changeover: a 17:30 early sitting with a 90-minute hold frees the table at 19:00, but needs a 10 to 15 minute reset before the next booking.
- Blackout dates (private hire, public holidays with surcharges, closures) override everything.

## Large groups

- Threshold: the venue's figure. Common practice is 8 or more.
- Apply the venue's set menu, deposit or card guarantee and cancellation window exactly as written. Do not invent a deposit amount, percentage or deadline.
- Spread large groups across pacing slots: two groups of 8 should not both arrive at 19:00 if pacing is 16.
- Groups over the venue's limit for the main room go to `function-enquiry-quote`.

## Alternatives

- Offer at most three times, within an hour of the request, earliest first.
- Prefer an earlier time over a later one for families with children, and a later time for pre-theatre only if the guest has time to make the show.
- If nothing fits within an hour, offer the waitlist and the nearest other service.

## Waitlist priority

1. Party size fits the freed table without a combination.
2. The guest's acceptable window includes the freed time.
3. Earliest added.
Give a reply deadline: 15 minutes on the day, 2 hours otherwise.

## Edge cases

- Duplicate requests from the same guest on two channels: one booking, reply on the channel they used most recently.
- Third-party bookings (a PA booking for a boss): confirm to the booker and record the guest name; reminders go to the booker unless told otherwise.
- Repeated no-shows (2 or more recorded): the manager decides whether to require a card guarantee; do not refuse a booking on your own judgement.
- A guest who mentions a severe allergy (anaphylaxis): flag to the manager before confirming, so the kitchen can say whether it can cater.
