# Guest message templates

Use the venue's name and voice. Keep SMS under 300 characters. Fill only from the booking; cut any line you cannot fill accurately.

## Confirmation (SMS)

> [Venue]: you're booked for [covers] on [day date] at [time], under [name]. [Large group or deposit line, if applicable.] To change or cancel, reply here or call [phone].

## Confirmation (email)

Subject: Your booking at [venue], [day date] at [time]

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for booking with us. Your table for [covers] is confirmed for [day date] at [time].
>
> - Dietary notes: [as recorded]. We have passed them to the kitchen; our team will confirm what they can prepare, and we cannot guarantee any dish is free of allergens.
> - [Policy lines, quoted from the venue's policy: table time, large-group terms, cancellation window.]
> - Getting here: [address, parking or transport note from the venue]
>
> To change or cancel, reply to this email or call [phone].
>
> [Name], [venue]

## 24-hour reminder (SMS)

> [Venue]: reminder of your booking for [covers] tomorrow, [day], at [time]. Reply YES to confirm or call [phone] if plans have changed. Thanks, [venue].

## Waitlist offer (SMS)

> [Venue]: a table for [covers] has come up at [time] [today/on day]. Reply YES by [deadline] to take it. If we don't hear back, we'll offer it to the next guest.

## Request does not fit, with alternatives

> Thanks for your request for [covers] at [time] on [day]. That time is fully booked, but we can seat you at [alt 1], [alt 2] or [alt 3]. Or we can add you to the waitlist for [time]. Let us know what suits.

## Deposit or card guarantee request (large groups, per venue policy)

> To hold your table for [covers] on [day date], our policy for groups of [threshold] or more is [policy wording exactly as written by the venue]. [Payment link or instructions supplied by the venue.] Your booking is held until [deadline from policy].

## Allergy acknowledgement

> Thanks for letting us know about the [allergy]. We've noted it on your booking and passed it to the kitchen. Our chefs will let you know what they can prepare. We take care, but we cannot guarantee any dish is free of allergens.

## Never write

- "Allergen-free", "100% gluten free", "safe for coeliacs", "nut-free kitchen", unless the venue manager supplies that exact wording and confirms it.
- A deposit amount, cancellation fee or table-time limit that is not in the venue's written policy.
- Promotional content in a reminder unless the guest has consented to marketing.
