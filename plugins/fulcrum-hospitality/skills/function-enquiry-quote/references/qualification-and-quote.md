# Qualification, quoting and follow-up

## Qualification questions

Ask in this order. Essentials first, in one message. Do not ask for anything already in the enquiry.

**Essentials (needed to quote)**
1. Date, and a back-up date
2. Guest numbers: expected, minimum and maximum
3. Event type and occasion
4. Style: seated meal, cocktail or canapés, buffet, grazing, drop-off catering
5. Start and finish time
6. Budget per head or total, or the spend they have in mind

**Second round (needed to finalise)**
7. Dietary requirements and allergies, with counts
8. AV: microphone, screen and projector, music, speeches
9. Accessibility needs
10. Entertainment, theming, cake, decorations, suppliers arriving
11. Children and their meals
12. Who is paying, invoice details, purchase order number for corporate clients
13. Decision date and who else is deciding

## Qualifying out

Say no early and politely when:
- Numbers exceed the largest room's capacity for the style.
- The budget sits below the minimum spend by more than a third and the client will not move date or day.
- The finish time is past licensed hours or a noise curfew.
- The date is taken and no back-up date suits.
Offer an alternative (another day, a smaller room, drop-off catering) before declining.

## Building the quote

| Line | Calculation | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food package | per-head price x guaranteed guests | Function pack |
| Beverage package | per-head price for the duration x adult guests | Function pack |
| Children's meals | per-child price x children | Function pack |
| Room hire | flat fee, or nil when minimum spend applies | Function pack |
| Minimum spend shortfall | minimum spend minus food and beverage subtotal, if positive | Function pack |
| AV and extras | item prices | Function pack or supplier quote |
| Surcharges | the venue's stated surcharge on the stated base | Terms |
| GST | as the price list presents it (inclusive or exclusive) | Price list |

Rules:
- Guaranteed numbers are the minimum the client will pay for, per the venue's terms.
- Round only at the line total, not per head.
- If a price is "from" in the pack, quote the specific option chosen, never the "from" figure.
- Show quote version, issue date and expiry.

## Deposit and terms

Copy the venue's own wording for:
- Deposit to confirm (amount or percentage, and due date)
- Final numbers deadline
- Final payment date and method
- Cancellation scale and what the deposit covers
- What happens when numbers fall below the guarantee
- Tentative hold period and what releases it

If the venue has no written term for one of these, leave it out of the quote and list it for the events manager.

## Follow-up cadence

| Day | Action | Content that adds value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Quote sent | Quote, menu, floor plan or photos of the room |
| 2 | Check-in email or call | Answer a likely question: dietary options, parking, AV, timeline |
| 5 | Hold-expiry reminder | Hold expires on [date]; offer a site visit or a call |
| Expiry | Release hold (manager approves) | Polite release; offer to re-check the date if they decide later |
| 30 after release | Optional, only if the client agreed to hear from the venue | New package or a date that has opened up |

Stop the cadence immediately when the client books, declines, asks you to stop or goes with another venue. Record the reason for lost enquiries: price, date, capacity, style, no response.

## Confirmed event checklist

- [ ] Deposit received (confirmed by the venue's records)
- [ ] Diary changed from tentative to confirmed
- [ ] BEO drafted and shared internally
- [ ] Final numbers reminder scheduled for [deadline minus 3 days]
- [ ] Supplier arrivals and access confirmed
- [ ] Final invoice drafted for approval after final numbers
