# Stay message templates

Replace every bracket from the booking, the property fact sheet or the policy. If a line cannot be filled from those sources, delete it. Sign off with a real person's first name or the front office team, as the property prefers.

## Pre-arrival (email, 3 to 7 days before)

Subject: Your stay at [property], [arrival date]

> Hi [first name],
>
> We're looking forward to welcoming you on [arrival day date] for [n] nights in a [room type].
>
> - Check-in is from [time]. If you'll arrive outside [front desk hours], let us know your arrival time and we'll arrange access.
> - Parking: [from fact sheet].
> - [Occasion line, only if the guest mentioned one.]
>
> Is there anything we can prepare for you? [One or two upsells from the approved list, for example: "A late check-out until [time] is available for [price] on your departure day, subject to availability."]
>
> [Name], [property] | [phone]
> [Unsubscribe line, if the email includes an offer]

## Check-in details and access (SMS, day before or morning of arrival)

> Hi [first name], [property] here. You're checking in today from [time]. [Access instructions from the fact sheet; codes only for the booked guest's verified number.] Questions? Call [phone].

## Welcome and settle-in (SMS, 1 to 2 hours after check-in)

> Hi [first name], welcome to [property]. Is everything OK with your room? Reply here if you need anything. [Wi-Fi or one useful fact from the fact sheet.]

## Request acknowledgement

> Thanks [first name], we've passed your request for [item] to [team] and expect to have it to room [number] by [time]. We'll let you know when it's done.

## Amendment within policy

> Hi [first name], we've changed your booking to [new dates], [room type], [rate]. [Price difference, if any, from the booking system.] Your updated confirmation is attached.

## Amendment or cancellation outside policy

> Hi [first name], thanks for letting us know. Your booking is on our [rate name] rate, which [policy wording exactly as written, for example: "is non-refundable" / "has a cancellation fee of ... within ... days of arrival"]. [What the policy allows, for example a date change.] [If a manager has approved a waiver: the approved outcome.] If you'd like to talk it through, call [name] on [phone].

## Departure reminder (SMS, evening before)

> Hi [first name], a reminder that check-out is [time] tomorrow. [Late check-out offer, if approved and available.] Safe travels from [property].

## Thank-you (email, day after departure)

> Hi [first name], thank you for staying with us at [property]. [One specific detail from the stay, if recorded.] We hope to see you again.

## Review request (email, 1 to 3 days after departure)

> Hi [first name], if you have a moment, we'd value your feedback on your stay: [review link]. Every review helps us improve, and we read them all. [Name], [property]

Send the same request to every departed guest. Where a complaint is unresolved, the manager follows up first and the review request goes afterwards. Offer nothing in exchange for a review.

## Complaint acknowledgement

> Hi [first name], I'm sorry about [issue in the guest's words]. [What we are doing now and when.] I'll check back with you by [time]. [Name], [role], [property]
