# Complaint recovery

Most guests who complain during a stay and see it fixed quickly rate the stay well. The goal is to fix the problem while the guest is still there, and to give the manager what they need when it cannot be fixed.

## Steps

1. **Acknowledge within the response standard** (default 15 minutes in staffed hours). Use the guest's words for the issue. Apologise for the experience without arguing about fault.
2. **Classify and route.**

| Type | Examples | Route to | Target fix time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Safety or security | Lock fault, smoke alarm, injury, unwelcome person | Duty manager now | Immediate |
| Room condition | Cleanliness, smell, pests, broken fixture | Housekeeping or maintenance | 30 to 60 minutes, or room move |
| Noise | Neighbours, plant, construction | Front office | 30 minutes, or room move |
| Service | Wait times, staff conduct | Duty manager | Same shift |
| Billing | Wrong charge, deposit not returned | Front office manager | 1 business day |
| Booking or policy | Wrong room type, cancellation fee dispute | Front office manager | Same day |

3. **Fix and confirm.** Tell the guest what is happening and when, then confirm when it is done. A promise without a check-back is a second complaint waiting to happen.
4. **Offer recovery within authority.** Typical staff authority (replace with the property's own): a room move to the same or a better type if available, a complimentary drink or breakfast, a late check-out. Credits, refunds and rate reductions go to the manager.
5. **Escalate** when: the guest asks for a refund or compensation; the issue involves safety, injury, discrimination or staff conduct; the guest mentions a review, media or legal action; or the same issue has occurred twice in the stay.
6. **Close and log**: issue, time reported, time fixed, recovery offered, guest response, owner. Repeated issues in the same room go to maintenance as a defect.

## Escalation note for the manager

> Guest: [name], room [number], [dates], booked via [channel]
> Issue: [in the guest's words], reported [time]
> Done so far: [actions and times]
> Guest's request: [what they asked for]
> Recommendation: [option within policy], [cost or impact]
> Decision needed by: [time]

## What not to do

- Do not argue with the guest's account or ask them to prove it.
- Do not blame other staff, contractors or other guests by name.
- Do not promise a refund, credit or free night without the manager's approval.
- Do not send the review request before the manager's follow-up, and do not drop the guest from review requests because they complained.
- Do not discuss another guest's details, even when they caused the problem.
