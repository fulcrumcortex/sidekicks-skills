# Review a business document

Identify the document, audience and requested review: clarity, completeness, consistency or a particular decision. Read the relevant full sections rather than isolated search hits. Confirm the version before editing a shared document.

Distinguish source facts, assumptions and recommendations. Check names, amounts, dates and cross-references against the available authoritative material. Flag unsupported assertions instead of manufacturing evidence or treating persuasive copy as a verified result.

Make concrete revisions that preserve the author's intent. Use comments or suggested text when a rewrite is not authorised. Keep unresolved questions visible and avoid changing contract meaning, financial conclusions or technical specifications silently.

If editing is authorised, change the intended document only and preserve available revision history. Check formatting, tables and links in the resulting view. Wait for the saved/synchronised state; visible edits alone do not prove persistence.

Return the major findings, revised passages or document link, and remaining questions. A writing review does not certify legal, financial or engineering suitability.
