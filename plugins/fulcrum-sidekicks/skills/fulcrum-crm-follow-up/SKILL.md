# Prepare a customer follow-up

Confirm the organisation and intended contact using stable details such as a record ID or email. Read the relevant timeline, company and open deal without retrieving unrelated customer data. Resolve ambiguous matches before changing or contacting anyone.

Identify the last agreed action, open questions and any existing task or recent send that could make a new follow-up a duplicate. Separate recorded facts from inference. Use current product or pricing sources for a claim that could have changed.

Draft a concise reply with the agreed next step and the right sender. Preserve suppression and consent preferences. Use an existing booking link or verified availability rather than inventing a meeting time.

If sending or updating records is explicitly authorised, check recipient, content, selected mailbox and current state immediately before the action. Otherwise return the draft and proposed changes. Creating a draft does not authorise a send.

Verify the sent item or saved task in the destination, recording a record link and next owner where available. After a timeout, inspect the send or activity history before retrying. Report draft, send, record update and delivery status distinctly.
