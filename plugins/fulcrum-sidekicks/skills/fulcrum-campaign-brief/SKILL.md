# Prepare a grounded campaign brief

Identify the audience, useful outcome, offer, channel and intended destination. Confirm the current product facts and the source for prices, availability and customer claims. Use existing brand guidance; do not invent testimonials, performance figures or working integrations.

Build a concise message around a real task or customer problem. Explain what the reader can do next and link to the relevant product, guide or skill rather than repeating a generic keyword anchor everywhere.

Prepare channel-specific copy and a simple asset list. Use fictional or approved demonstration data in visuals. Keep credentials, customer records and internal operations out of public material. Distinguish an illustration, a demo and a real customer result.

Select meaningful measures such as qualified responses, confirmed bookings or task completion. A published backlink or page view is not proof of ranking improvement or revenue. Do not promise indexing, compliance or fixed outcomes.

Deliver the reviewable brief and drafts. If publication is already authorised, verify the target account, final copy and channel constraints, publish through supported controls and record the public URLs. Do not create accounts, buy distribution or broaden the campaign without authority. Reconcile timeouts before duplicate posting.
