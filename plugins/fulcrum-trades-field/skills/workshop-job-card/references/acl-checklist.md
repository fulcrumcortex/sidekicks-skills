# Australian Consumer Law checklist for workshops

Run this at intake, when authorising extra work, and at handover. It is a practical aid for a service adviser; the workshop's own legal advice governs.

## At intake

- [ ] **Repair notice.** Under the ACL and the Competition and Consumer Regulations, a repairer must give the customer a written notice before accepting goods for repair if:
  - the workshop may replace the goods or parts with refurbished ones instead of repairing them, or uses refurbished parts in repairs; or
  - the goods can hold user-generated data (paired phones, contacts, saved destinations, driver profiles), in which case the notice warns that the repair may result in loss of that data.
  The regulations set the wording. Use the workshop's prescribed notice; do not paraphrase it.
- [ ] Existing damage, fuel level and odometer are recorded with photos.
- [ ] The authorised scope and amount are written on the card and agreed with the customer.

## Consumer guarantees that apply

A customer is generally a "consumer" for goods or services costing up to $100,000, or of a kind ordinarily acquired for personal, domestic or household use, and for vehicles used mainly to transport goods on public roads.

Services guarantees:

- [ ] Provided with due care and skill.
- [ ] Fit for any purpose the customer made known (for example "fix the noise before a long trip").
- [ ] Provided within a reasonable time when no time was agreed.

Parts supplied guarantees:

- [ ] Of acceptable quality.
- [ ] Fit for any disclosed purpose.
- [ ] Match their description (genuine versus aftermarket, new versus refurbished).

These guarantees cannot be excluded. Do not write "no warranty", "no refunds" or "all care no responsibility" on job cards or invoices.

## When something goes wrong after the job

- [ ] A minor failure: the workshop may fix it within a reasonable time.
- [ ] A major failure (for example a fault a reasonable customer would not have accepted, or the vehicle is unsafe): the customer may be entitled to cancel and get a refund for the service or compensation for the reduced value.
- [ ] Record the complaint, the inspection and the remedy offered on the original job card's history.

## Warranties the workshop gives

- [ ] If the workshop offers its own warranty against defects, the document must include the mandatory text set by the regulations and the workshop's details. Use the workshop's approved wording.
- [ ] Servicing by an independent workshop does not, by itself, void a new-car manufacturer's warranty when it is done to the manufacturer's specifications with parts of suitable quality. Record the parts and specifications used and stamp the logbook.

## Authorisation records

- [ ] Each additional item has a written approval with the amount, who approved, how and when.
- [ ] Work stopped when the cost would exceed the approved amount, and re-authorised.
- [ ] Declined safety items recorded with the warning given and the customer's response.

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.
