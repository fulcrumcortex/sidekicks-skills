# Incident screen

Run this on every note before handing it back. If any line matches, add an Incident flag block at the top of the note and tell the author to follow the provider's incident management process now. If someone may be in immediate danger, call 000 first. The agent flags; the provider decides whether something is reportable and to whom.

## Possible NDIS reportable incident (registered providers)

Flag if the source describes or suggests, involving a participant in connection with the supports:

- [ ] Death of a participant
- [ ] Serious injury
- [ ] Abuse or neglect
- [ ] Unlawful sexual or physical contact with, or assault of, a participant
- [ ] Sexual misconduct committed against, or in the presence of, a participant
- [ ] Use of a restrictive practice that was not authorised, or not in line with a behaviour support plan (for example locking a door, holding someone, giving a medication to control behaviour, removing items)

Registered providers must notify the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission within set timeframes, which are short (most categories within 24 hours of key personnel becoming aware, some within 5 business days). Check the current timeframes in the Commission's guidance and the provider's own procedure.

## Other incidents to record under the provider's process

- [ ] Fall, injury or near miss, even if the person says they are fine
- [ ] Medication error or missed medication
- [ ] Missing person or unexplained absence
- [ ] Property damage, vehicle incident or hazard at the location
- [ ] Threat or harm to a worker
- [ ] Disclosure by the participant about harm by anyone, including outside the service
- [ ] A complaint by the participant or family
- [ ] Concern about a child's safety (mandatory reporting rules for the state may apply)
- [ ] A privacy breach, such as a note or message sent to the wrong person

## How to write the flag

- One line that names the matching item and quotes the minimum source words needed.
- Do not speculate about cause, fault or intent.
- Do not include the flag in anything sent outside the provider.
- Keep the progress note factual; the incident report is a separate record written under the provider's process.

This is a practical checklist, not legal or clinical advice.
