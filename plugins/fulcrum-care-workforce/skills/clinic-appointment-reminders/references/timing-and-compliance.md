# Timing, cadence and compliance

Read this when agreeing a cadence and before every approval gate.

## Reminder cadence

| Situation | Touches | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard consult booked within 4 weeks | Confirmation, reminder, follow-up if no reply | At booking; 2 business days before; day before 10:00 to 16:00 |
| Booked more than 4 weeks ahead | Add a 7-day reminder | 7 days before |
| Long or prepared appointment (procedure, fasting, equipment) | Add preparation email and a confirmation call if no reply | 7 days before for preparation; call the day before |
| Monday appointment | Reminder on the previous Thursday, follow-up on Friday | Avoid weekend sends for routine reminders |
| Same-day booking | Confirmation only | At booking |
| Early-morning appointment (before 9:00) | Day-before reminder | Not on the morning itself |

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays in the clinic's state.

## Recall cadence (routine only)

| Step | Timing | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 weeks before the due date | SMS or email |
| 2 | 3 weeks after step 1 if not booked | SMS or email |
| 3 | 3 weeks after step 2 | Staff or voice agent call |
| Close | After step 3 | Record "recall closed, no response"; review at the next due date |

Clinical recall (abnormal results, missed critical follow-up) is not run by this skill. It goes to the responsible clinician with every attempt documented.

## Calling hours

- Place reminder and confirmation calls within the telemarketing industry standard hours as good practice even when the call is not telemarketing: weekdays 9:00 to 20:00 and Saturday 9:00 to 17:00, not Sunday or national public holidays. Many clinics use a narrower window, such as 10:00 to 16:00, for confirmations.
- Hours apply in the patient's local time. A tool whose window is set in Sydney time can call a Perth patient at 6:00 local time unless you narrow it.
- Send SMS in similar hours. Late-night reminders produce complaints and opt-outs.

## Compliance checklist

Spam Act 2003:

- [ ] Pure appointment reminders and confirmations are factual messages; still identify the clinic in every one
- [ ] Recall that promotes a service, and any newsletter or offer, is commercial: consent (express, or inferred from an existing relationship), the clinic identified, and a working unsubscribe honoured within 5 working days
- [ ] Opt-outs recorded and applied to every future batch

Do Not Call Register:

- [ ] Calls about an existing appointment are not telemarketing. If calls promote services to people who are not current patients, wash the list against the Register yourself or through an access seeker first, unless an exemption applies. No Fulcrum product washes numbers against the Register.

Calls, AI and recording:

- [ ] Every AI call discloses at the start that the caller is an AI and that the call may be recorded
- [ ] Recording consent rules vary by state; the disclosure covers recording
- [ ] Requests not to be called are recorded and honoured across all campaigns and tasks

Privacy Act and the Australian Privacy Principles:

- [ ] Messages contain only first name, clinic, date, time and place
- [ ] Numbers verified; shared or uncertain numbers get a call instead of a detailed SMS
- [ ] Minors' messages go to the parent or guardian recorded on file, considering the young person's own privacy where they manage their own care
- [ ] Patient lists stay in the clinic's systems and are not pasted into public tools
- [ ] A message sent to the wrong person is handled as a possible privacy breach under the clinic's process

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.
