# Message templates

Read this when drafting a batch. Keep every SMS under 160 characters where possible, so it sends as one part. Replace `[Clinic]` with the neutral sender name for sensitive services (for example "Harbour Health" rather than a name that reveals the specialty). All names and numbers are fictional.

## SMS

Booking confirmation:

> Hi [first name], your appointment at [Clinic] is booked for [Tue 12 Nov] at [9:30am], [address or "by video"]. To change it call [0400 000 000].

Reminder, 2 business days before:

> Hi [first name], reminder of your appointment at [Clinic] on [Tue 12 Nov] at [9:30am]. Reply Y to confirm or N to cancel.

Second reminder, day before, no reply yet:

> Hi [first name], we haven't heard back about [tomorrow] at [9:30am] with [Clinic]. Reply Y to confirm or N to cancel, or call [0400 000 000].

Preparation needed:

> Hi [first name], your appointment at [Clinic] on [Tue 12 Nov] at [9:30am] needs some preparation. Please check your email, or call [0400 000 000].

Cancellation received:

> Thanks [first name], your [Tue 12 Nov] appointment is cancelled. To book a new time: [link] or call [0400 000 000].

No-show (within 2 hours, no risk flag):

> Hi [first name], we missed you at [Clinic] today. If you'd like a new time: [link] or call [0400 000 000].

Waitlist offer:

> Hi [first name], [Clinic] has an earlier appointment on [Thu 7 Nov] at [2:00pm]. Reply YES by [3:15pm] to take it. It's yours once we confirm.

Waitlist slot taken:

> Thanks [first name], that time has now gone. You're still on our waitlist.

Routine recall, first message:

> Hi [first name], you're due for your [routine review] with [Clinic]. Book at [link] or call [0400 000 000]. Reply STOP to opt out of recall messages.

Clinical or urgent reply received (send once, then alert staff):

> Thanks, a member of our team will contact you. If you need urgent help, call 000.

## Voicemail (minimal)

> Hi, this is a message for [first name] from [Clinic]. Please call us on [0400 000 000]. Thank you.

Never leave the appointment type, practitioner specialty or reason on a voicemail.

## Voice agent confirmation call script

Use as the objective's goal, information to collect and guardrails.

1. Disclosure: "Hi, this is [agent name], an AI assistant calling from [Clinic]. This call may be recorded."
2. Identity: "Am I speaking with [first name]?" If not: "No problem. Could you ask [first name] to call [Clinic] on [number]? Thanks." End the call.
3. Purpose: "I'm calling about your appointment on [day date] at [time] at [location]. Will you be able to make it?"
4. Collect one of: confirmed, cancel, wants to reschedule, unsure.
5. Cancel or reschedule: offer the booking link by SMS if the patient agrees, or say reception will call back. Do not move the booking on the call unless the booking tool confirms it.
6. Guardrails: never discuss the reason for the visit, results, medication or fees; on any clinical question, distress or a request for a person, say a team member will call back and mark Escalated to human; if the person asks not to be called again, confirm and record it.
7. Close: "Thanks [first name]. See you on [day]."

## Email (preparation or recall detail)

Email can carry more detail than SMS, but it is still outside the clinic's systems. Send preparation instructions to the address on file only, use the subject "Your appointment with [Clinic]", and keep clinical information to what the patient needs to prepare.
