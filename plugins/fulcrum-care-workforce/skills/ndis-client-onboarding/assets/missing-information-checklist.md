# Missing-information checklist

Participant reference: [P-xxxx]  Prepared: [date]  Status: [not ready / ready for review]

## From the participant or their representative

- [ ] Who will sign, and evidence of authority if not the participant
- [ ] Preferred contact method, and whether a support person or interpreter is wanted
- [ ] Copy or extract of the current plan showing dates and funding type per budget
- [ ] Goals in their own words
- [ ] Budget they want to allocate to these supports
- [ ] Session days, times and location; anything at the location that affects safety
- [ ] Consent choices (each optional consent answered yes or no)

## From the plan manager (plan-managed budgets only)

- [ ] Plan manager organisation name and invoice email
- [ ] Their invoice requirements (item numbers, NDIS number, date format)

## From the provider

- [ ] ABN and, if registered, registration number
- [ ] Support item numbers confirmed against the current Support Catalogue
- [ ] Agreed prices confirmed at or below the current price limit for the location (NDIA-managed and plan-managed)
- [ ] Cancellation policy wording consistent with the current Pricing Arrangements
- [ ] Complaints contact and current NDIS Commission contact details
- [ ] GST treatment confirmed
- [ ] For NDIA-managed budgets: provider link in the portal confirmed
