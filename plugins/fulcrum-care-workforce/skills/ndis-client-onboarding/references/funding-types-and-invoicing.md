# Funding management types and how they change invoicing

Read this when choosing the invoicing route for each budget in a participant's plan. Confirm details against the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits and the NDIA's provider guidance; rules change, usually from 1 July.

## Decision table

| | NDIA-managed (agency-managed) | Plan-managed | Self-managed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who pays the provider | The NDIA, from the participant's plan | The participant's plan manager | The participant or their nominee, who then claims from the NDIA |
| Provider must be NDIS-registered | Yes | No | No |
| Price limits apply | Yes | Yes | No, but record the agreed price in the agreement |
| How you get paid | A payment request (claim) in the NDIA provider portal, one by one or by bulk upload | An invoice to the plan manager | An invoice to the participant or nominee |
| What must be in place first | Agreement, and the provider link the portal requires for that plan (a service booking on older plans, or the participant's provider endorsement under the NDIA's newer system; check which applies) | Agreement, plan manager's invoice address and their invoice requirements | Agreement, and who receives invoices |
| Common failure | Claiming before the provider link exists, or after the plan ends | Invoice missing the support item number or service dates | Invoices sent to the wrong person when a nominee handles payments |

## Mixed plans

A plan can mix types by budget, for example Core supports NDIA-managed and Capacity Building plan-managed. Split the schedule of supports by budget and state the route on each line. Never move a cost from one budget to another to make the numbers fit.

## What a plan-managed or self-managed invoice usually needs

Plan managers set their own requirements; ask for them. Most expect:

- provider legal name and ABN
- participant name and NDIS number (only on the invoice, not in emails)
- invoice number and date
- service date or date range for each line
- support item number and name, as in the current Support Catalogue
- quantity (hours as decimals, for example 1.25), unit price and line total
- GST treatment for each line
- payment details

## GST

Many NDIS supplies are GST-free only when specific conditions are met, including a written agreement with the participant or their nominee that identifies the participant's plan. Put the plan reference in the agreement and ask the provider to confirm GST treatment with their accountant.

## Price limits and location

- Price limits differ by location. Some areas attract a remote or very remote loading where the Pricing Arrangements apply one. Use the participant's service location, not the provider's office.
- When price limits change, an existing agreement does not update itself. The agreement should say how price changes are handled and how much notice the participant gets.
- Self-managed participants can agree a price above a price limit, but the participant must understand the effect on their budget. Record it plainly.

## Before the first claim or invoice

- [ ] Agreement signed by the person with authority
- [ ] Funding type confirmed per budget, from the plan rather than from memory
- [ ] Plan start and end dates recorded; no supports scheduled after the end date
- [ ] Provider link in place for NDIA-managed budgets
- [ ] Plan manager details and invoice requirements on file for plan-managed budgets
- [ ] Item numbers and prices verified against the current catalogue

This is a practical checklist, not legal or financial advice.
