# What goes in the pack, and the compliance checks

Read this when drafting the service agreement and consent forms, and before the approval gate.

## Service agreement: clauses to include

| Clause | What it says | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parties | Participant, signer and authority, provider legal name, ABN, registration status | Never invent an ABN or registration number |
| Plan | Plan reference and dates the agreement relies on | Agreement end date should not pass the plan end date without a review clause |
| Supports | What will be delivered, where, how often, by whom (role, not name) | Link each support to a plan goal in the participant's words |
| Schedule of supports | Item number, unit, agreed price, quantity, budget, funding type | Every `[VERIFY]` resolved before signing |
| Invoicing and payment | Route per budget, invoice timing, who receives invoices | Mixed plans need a line per budget |
| Cancellations and no-shows | Notice the participant gives, what can be charged, consistent with the current Pricing Arrangements | Do not charge more than the Pricing Arrangements allow for NDIA-managed or plan-managed budgets |
| Provider travel and non-face-to-face time | Whether they are charged, and how | Only with the participant's agreement and where allowed |
| Participant responsibilities | Tell the provider about changes, give notice of cancellations, provide a safe environment | Keep them reasonable and short |
| Provider responsibilities | Deliver supports safely and competently, communicate in the participant's preferred way, protect privacy | Mirror the NDIS Code of Conduct |
| Changes and ending | How either party changes or ends the agreement, and the notice period | No exit fees or lock-in |
| Feedback and complaints | How to raise a concern with the provider, and that they can contact the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission | Give the Commission's name; the user adds current contact details |
| Privacy | What is collected, why, who it is shared with, how to access or correct it | Point to the consent form |
| Signatures | Participant or authorised representative, provider, date | Record how authority was confirmed |

## Consent forms: rules

- One consent per purpose. Do not combine consent to receive services with optional consents.
- Name the people or organisations information may be shared with. "Other providers as needed" is not specific enough.
- Make optional items (photos, recordings, case studies, AI transcription) opt-in and unticked.
- State how to withdraw consent and that withdrawing optional consent does not affect services.
- For a nominee, guardian or parent, record the basis of their authority and still involve the participant as far as possible.

## Compliance checklist for the pack

NDIS Code of Conduct and Practice Standards:

- [ ] The participant's choice and control is respected: supports, times and worker preferences come from them
- [ ] Information is in a form the participant understands (plain English, Easy Read, interpreter, or support person present)
- [ ] The agreement explains how to make a complaint and that doing so will not affect their services
- [ ] Prices and charges are transparent and match the schedule
- [ ] No conflict of interest is hidden, for example where the provider also offers support coordination

Privacy Act and the Australian Privacy Principles:

- [ ] Only information needed for the agreement is collected (health and disability information is sensitive information)
- [ ] The participant knows why it is collected, who sees it, and how to access or correct it
- [ ] Files are stored in the provider's own restricted storage, not personal drives or public tools
- [ ] Documents are sent by a restricted method, to the confirmed recipient only
- [ ] Old drafts with participant details are deleted or kept only in the participant's file

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.
