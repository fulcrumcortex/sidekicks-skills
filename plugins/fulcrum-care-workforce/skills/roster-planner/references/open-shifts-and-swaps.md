# Open shifts, swaps and sick calls

Read this when a shift has no worker, someone asks to swap, or a worker calls in sick.

## Open shift: offer order

1. The client's regular team (continuity of care).
2. Part-time workers below their contracted hours.
3. Casual workers who are available and eligible.
4. Workers who would go into overtime, only with the roster owner's approval.
5. Agency staff, with the agency confirming screening and training for this client.

Offer only to workers the checker lists as eligible. A worker who is not screened, trained or available never sees the offer.

## How to offer

| Notice before shift | Method | Response window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More than 48 hours | Targeted offer to the top candidate, then the next | 2 hours each during business hours |
| 24 to 48 hours | Offer to the top three eligible candidates at once; first to accept, confirmed by the coordinator | 1 hour |
| Less than 24 hours | Phone the top candidates; send a message only as a follow-up | Immediate |

Tell the others promptly once it is filled.

## Message templates

Shift offer (no client health details):

> Hi [first name], a shift is available: [day date], [start] to [end], [suburb], [support type, for example personal care]. Reply YES to accept or NO to pass. It is confirmed only when [coordinator] replies. [Organisation name]

Filled notice:

> Thanks [first name], the [day] [start] shift in [suburb] has been filled. [Organisation name]

Client change notice (after approval):

> Hi [client or representative first name], [worker first name] will support you on [day] at [time] instead of [usual worker first name]. If you would prefer a different time or worker, call [number]. [Organisation name]

## Swap requests

Approve a swap only when all of these hold:

- [ ] The incoming worker passes every hard constraint for the shift (rerun the checker with the swap applied)
- [ ] No new double booking, short rest or overtime is created, or the owner approves it
- [ ] The client has not excluded the incoming worker; tell the client if their usual worker is changing
- [ ] Both workers confirm in writing
- [ ] The roster and timesheets are updated with the reason

## Sick call or same-day gap

1. Thank the worker; do not ask for medical details beyond what policy requires.
2. List the affected shifts for the day and triage by client risk:
   - First: personal care, medication support, meals for clients who cannot prepare them, clients living alone, clients with high-intensity supports.
   - Then: domestic assistance and therapy that can safely move.
   - Last: community access and social supports, which may be rescheduled with the client's agreement.
3. Phone eligible candidates in the offer order above.
4. If a high-risk shift cannot be covered, escalate to the roster owner immediately and agree a safety plan with the client or their family. Record the decision.
5. Tell each affected client before the shift, after approval of the message.
6. Note any shift cancelled by the provider; it is never charged to the client.
