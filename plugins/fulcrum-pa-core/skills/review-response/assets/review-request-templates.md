# Review request templates

Use after the checklist in references/requesting-reviews.md passes. Fill every bracket. Send the same request to every customer in the chosen group, whatever you expect their view to be. Each send needs approval of the exact text, recipients and sending account.

## Email

Subject: How did we do, [first name]?

Hi [first name],

Thanks for choosing [business] for [service, in general terms]. We'd value your honest feedback, good or bad. If you have a minute, you can leave a review here: [review link]

Leaving a review is entirely optional.

Thanks,
[name], [business]
[business address or phone]

[Unsubscribe link]

## SMS

[Business]: thanks for choosing us, [first name]. We'd value your honest review: [short link]. Optional, and thank you. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Reminder (one only, 5 to 7 days later, only if no review and no opt-out)

[Business]: a last reminder, [first name]: if you'd like to share feedback on your recent visit, here's the link: [short link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

## In person or on a call

"If you have a moment later, we'd really value an honest review. I can text you the link, if that's OK. It's completely up to you."

## Confidential services (clinic, allied health, disability support)

Keep the request generic. Do not name the treatment, support or appointment type in the message, as someone else may read it.

Subject: Your feedback for [business]

Hi [first name], we'd welcome your honest feedback about [business]. If you'd like to, you can leave a review here: [link]. It's optional and won't affect your [care / services] in any way. To protect your privacy, please avoid including health or personal details in a public review. [Unsubscribe link]
