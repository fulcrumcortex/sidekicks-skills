# Scheduling message templates

Keep them short. Replace everything in angle brackets. Every message goes through the approval gate: show the final text, recipients and sending account, then wait for a yes.

## Propose times (external)

Subject: <Topic>: a few times for <duration> minutes

Hi <First name>,

Would one of these suit for <purpose>? All times are <recipient's city> time.

- <Weekday date>, <start> to <end>
- <Weekday date>, <start> to <end>
- <Weekday date>, <start> to <end>

I'll send an invitation with a video link once you pick one. If none of these work, <booking link or "send me a couple of times that do">.

<Owner name>

## Propose times (group, several zones)

Hi all,

Here are three options for <purpose> (<duration> minutes):

| Option | <Zone A city> | <Zone B city> | <Zone C city> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <Tue 10 Nov 11:00> | <Tue 10 Nov 10:00> | <Tue 10 Nov 08:00> |
| 2 | ... | ... | ... |

Reply with every option you can make and I'll book the first one that suits everyone.

## Booking link

Hi <First name>,

Thanks for <reason>. The easiest way to find a time is to pick one here: <booking link>. It shows times in your own time zone and sends you a confirmation straight away.

## Confirmation after acceptance (sent with or just before the invitation)

Hi <First name>, booked for <weekday date>, <time> <recipient city> time. The invitation has the video link. <One line agenda, if agreed.>

## Reschedule requested by the owner

Hi <First name>,

Something has come up and I need to move our <weekday> meeting, sorry for the change. Would either of these work instead?

- <option 1, recipient's zone>
- <option 2, recipient's zone>

The current invitation stays in place until we confirm a new time.

## Reschedule requested by the guest

No problem at all. Would <option 1> or <option 2> work? <Or: you can pick a new time from the link in your confirmation email.>

## No-show, at +5 minutes

Hi <First name>, I'm in the meeting room now: <link>. Happy to wait a few minutes, or we can find another time.

## No-show follow-up, same day

Hi <First name>,

Sorry we missed each other today. If it's still useful to talk about <purpose>, here are two times: <option 1>, <option 2>. Or pick any time here: <booking link>.

## Time-zone change notice for a recurring meeting

Hi all, a heads-up: daylight saving starts in <state> on <date>, so from <date> our <weekday> meeting will be at <new local time> in <city> (it stays at <time> in <organiser city>).
