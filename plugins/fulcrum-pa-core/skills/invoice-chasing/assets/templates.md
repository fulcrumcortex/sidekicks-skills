# Invoice chasing templates

Fill every bracket. Remove any line that does not apply. Keep one clear ask per message. Sign as a named person, not "Accounts".

## Stage 0: courtesy reminder (optional, before due)

Subject: Invoice [number] due [day date]

Hi [first name],

A quick note that invoice [number] for $[amount] is due on [day date]. A copy is attached, and you can pay by [payment method, e.g. bank transfer to BSB [xxx-xxx] account [xxxxxxxx], reference [number], or the link on the invoice].

Thanks,
[name], [business] | [phone]

## Stage 1: friendly nudge (1 to 7 days overdue)

Subject: Invoice [number] - just a reminder

Hi [first name],

I think invoice [number] for $[amount], due [date], may have slipped through. I've attached a copy. If it's already on its way, thank you and please ignore this.

You can pay by [payment method]. If anything on the invoice needs fixing, just let me know.

Thanks,
[name], [business] | [phone]

SMS (only to a confirmed billing contact): [Business]: invoice [number] for $[amount] was due [date]. Copy and payment details: [link]. Already paid? Thanks, please ignore.

## Stage 2: firm reminder (8 to 14 days overdue)

Subject: Overdue: invoice [number], $[amount]

Hi [first name],

Invoice [number] for $[amount] is now [n] days overdue (due [date]). Could you reply with the date we can expect payment, or pay today by [payment method]?

If there's a problem with the invoice or the work, tell me and I'll sort it out with you.

Regards,
[name], [business] | [phone]

SMS: [Business]: invoice [number], $[amount], is [n] days overdue. Please reply with a payment date or call [phone]. Details: [link]

## Stage 3: call note (15 to 30 days overdue)

```text
Debtor: [business / person]      Contact: [name, role]      Number: [number]
Invoices: [number, amount, due date; ...]    Total overdue: $[amount]
Previous contact: [dates and stages]
Open with: who you are, the business, and that you're calling about invoice [number].
Ask: "Is there anything stopping this from being paid?" Then: "When can we expect payment?"
Can offer: [payment plan limits set by the user, e.g. up to 3 fortnightly instalments]
Do not: threaten, discuss with anyone else, agree to discounts the user has not approved.
Record: outcome (promise date / dispute / plan / no answer), next contact date.
Voicemail (neutral): "Hi, it's [name] from [business], please call me back on [phone] about your account. Thanks."
```

Email after the call: "Thanks for your time today. As discussed, you'll pay $[amount] for invoice [number] by [date]. Payment details: [method]."

## Stage 4: statement of account (31 to 45 days overdue)

Subject: Statement of account - [debtor] - $[total] overdue

Hi [first name],

Please find attached your statement as at [date]. The overdue balance is $[total]:

| Invoice | Date | Due | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [number] | [date] | [date] | $[amount] |

We haven't yet received payment or a payment date. Please pay by [date, 7 days out] or call me on [phone] to arrange it.

Regards,
[name], [business]

## Stage 5: final notice (46 to 60 days overdue)

Only after the user approves this debtor and the stated next step.

Subject: Final notice: $[total] overdue - please respond by [date]

Dear [name],

Despite our reminders on [dates], invoices [numbers] totalling $[total] remain unpaid. They are now [n] days overdue.

Please pay the full amount by [date, at least 7 days out]. If you can't pay in full, contact me before that date and we can discuss a payment plan.

If we don't receive payment or hear from you by [date], we will [the next step the user has decided on, e.g. refer the debt to a collection agency]. We'd prefer to resolve this with you directly.

Yours sincerely,
[name], [title], [business], ABN [ABN]

## Payment plan confirmation

Subject: Payment plan for invoices [numbers]

Hi [first name],

Thanks for working this out with us. To confirm the plan:

| Instalment | Amount | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | $[amount] | [date] |
| 2 | $[amount] | [date] |

Payment details: [method, reference]. If an instalment is going to be late, please let me know before its due date. [If agreed: If an instalment is missed without notice, the remaining balance becomes payable in full.]

Regards,
[name], [business]

## Reply to "we've already paid"

Hi [first name], thanks for letting me know. Could you send the remittance, or the date and amount of the payment and the account it came from? I'll match it and confirm. Apologies if our reminder crossed with your payment.

## Reply to a dispute

Hi [first name], thanks for raising this. I've put invoice [number] on hold while we look into it, so you won't receive further reminders about it in the meantime. [Name] will be in touch by [date] to work through [the issue].
