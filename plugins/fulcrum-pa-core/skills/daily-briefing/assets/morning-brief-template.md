# Morning brief template

Limits: about 25 lines, 200 words, no section longer than five lines. Short lines; the owner reads it on a phone. Use the owner's local time.

```
<Weekday d Mon> brief · <time zone abbreviation>
Outcomes this week: <1> · <2> · <3>

DECIDE
1. <Question?> <Options.> <Recommendation or "draft ready">
2. ...
3. ...

TODAY (<n> meetings)
<hh:mm> <Who, organisation> · <purpose> · <Ready|Partial: gap|None|Not needed>
...

WAITING ON YOU (<n>)
<Name, org> · <ask in 6 words> · <age>d <draft ready?>
...
+<n> more: <breakdown>

MONEY
In: <$received> received · <$due> due today
Overdue: <$total> across <n> (oldest <d>d)
Out: <$bills> bills due in 7d

RISKS
- <Risk> → <next action, owner>

HANDLED
<n> drafts ready · <n> meetings confirmed · <other>

Not included: <unavailable sources, or "all sources refreshed <hh:mm>">
```
