# Friday weekly review

Run on the owner's last working day of the week, mid-afternoon, so there is time to act on it. Target length: one phone screen plus the three outcomes, about 35 lines.

## 1. Wins (up to five)

Count something as a win only if it is recorded:

- Deals moved to won, with value.
- Payments received over the owner's threshold, or an overdue invoice finally paid.
- Projects, jobs or milestones completed.
- A complaint resolved, a strong review received, a key hire accepted.
- Last week's outcomes that were met (always report these first).

Write each win in one line with the number: "Won: Kestrel Fitout stage 2, $18,400".

## 2. Slipped (up to five)

Something slipped when a commitment existed and the date passed:

- Last week's three outcomes not met. Report each as met, partly met or missed, with the one-line reason.
- Tasks overdue more than 3 business days.
- Deals whose expected close date moved this week (say from and to).
- Replies the owner promised and has not sent.
- Invoices that crossed 30 or 60 days overdue this week.

No blame language. State the fact and the proposed recovery: "Missed: send Lakeside proposal. Cause: pricing waiting on supplier. Recovery: send Tuesday with provisional rates."

## 4. Next week's three outcomes

An outcome is a result, not an activity. Test each candidate:

- **Specific and checkable by Friday**: "Signed agreement from Lakeside" not "work on Lakeside".
- **Moves money, customers or capacity**: revenue, cash, retention, a hire, a system that saves time.
- **Mostly within the owner's control**.
- **One owner**, even if others help.

Draft five candidates from: slipped items, deals near close, the biggest overdue invoices, upcoming deadlines in mail and calendar, and the owner's quarterly goals. Present them ranked, with one line of reasoning each. The owner picks three; if they do not answer, carry the top three as proposed and label them "proposed, not confirmed".

## 5. Calendar check for next week

- Days with more than 5 hours of meetings.
- No focus block of at least 2 hours on any day.
- External meetings with prep status None.
- Travel or in-person meetings missing travel time.
- Daylight-saving changes in any attendee's zone (see `meeting-scheduler`).

## Rules

- Carry the chosen outcomes into Monday's brief header and check them in next Friday's review.
- If a source was unavailable during the week, say which days are incomplete.
