# Meeting brief template

About 400 words. Facts carry a source tag in brackets: [CRM], [email 3 Oct], [public: company site, read 9 Nov]. Inferences say "likely".

```
<Meeting title> · <Weekday d Mon hh:mm> <owner's zone> · <location or link>
Type: <discovery | proposal | negotiation | renewal | complaint | partner | supplier>

GOAL: <what must be true at the end>
NEXT STEP: <primary commitment, with date>  Fallback: <...>

WHO
| Name | Role | Relationship | Notes |
| <name> | <role> [source] | <since / first meeting> | <priorities, channel> |

HISTORY
- <date> <what happened> [source]
- ... (up to 5; broken promises by us first)

OPEN
Deal: <name> · <stage> · <$amount> · close <date> · last activity <n>d [CRM]
Issues: <complaint / ticket, status, age>
Money: <unpaid invoices and age, or "none">

ASK
1. <question>
... (5 to 7)

OBJECTIONS
- "<objection>" (evidence: <source>) → <response>
... (up to 3)

LANDMINES
- <what not to raise, or how to handle>

LOGISTICS
<link tested? address? parking? attendees' local time if different>

Sources: <list> · Not available: <missing sources>
```

## Worked example (fictional)

```
Proposal walk-through · Tue 10 Nov 09:30 AEDT · Fulcrum meeting link in invite
Type: proposal

GOAL: Priya confirms the scope and names any changes needed.
NEXT STEP: Verbal yes and signed acceptance by Fri 20 Nov.  Fallback: call with their practice manager booked by Wed 18 Nov.

WHO
| Priya Natarajan | Owner, Bluegum Dental (example.com) [CRM] | Since Aug, 3 meetings | Wants fit-out done before Jan closure [email 21 Oct] |
| Unknown | ops@ address, Bluegum Dental | Not in CRM | Possibly practice manager (inferred from address) |

HISTORY
- 21 Oct  Asked for staged option to spread cost [email]
- 28 Oct  Proposal v2 sent, $46,500 two stages [CRM]
- 30 Oct  We promised revised timeline; not yet sent [CRM note, Sam]
- 3 Nov   Priya: "budget is tight until Feb" [email]

OPEN
Deal: Bluegum fit-out · Proposal · $46,500 · close 20 Nov · last activity 7d [CRM]
Issues: none · Money: none owed

ASK
1. Has anything changed since the 3 Nov email?
2. Is the January closure date fixed?
3. Which stage matters most if we split the work?
4. Who else signs off, and does the practice manager need a walk-through?
5. What would you need to see to approve this week?

OBJECTIONS
- "Too expensive right now" (3 Nov email) → offer stage 1 in Jan, stage 2 after Feb; no discount without owner's limit.
- "Need to check with the practice manager" (ops@ on invite) → offer a 20-minute walk-through this week.

LANDMINES
- Revised timeline we promised on 30 Oct was not sent. Acknowledge it first and bring it.

LOGISTICS
Link in invite; both in Sydney time.

Sources: CRM, mail (90 days) · Not available: public research (not authorised)
```
