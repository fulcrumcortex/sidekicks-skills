# Speed-to-lead response drafts

Short, specific, one next step. Reply on the channel the person used. Every draft goes through the approval gate before it is sent. Replace angle brackets.

## Quote or price request (email)

Subject: Re: <their subject>

Hi <First name>,

Thanks for getting in touch about <need in their words>. To give you an accurate quote I need two things: <detail 1> and <detail 2>.

The quickest way is a short call. I can ring you <today at 2:30 or tomorrow at 9:00>, or you can pick a time here: <booking link>.

<Owner name>
<Business name> · <phone>

## Call-back request (SMS)

Hi <First name>, it's <owner name> from <business>. Thanks for your message about <need>. I'll call you at <time today>. If that doesn't suit, reply with a better time. Reply STOP to opt out.

(Include the opt-out line on any SMS that could be commercial. Use the business's registered sender name.)

## General enquiry

Hi <First name>,

Thanks for asking about <topic>. <Direct answer in one or two sentences.>

If it helps to talk it through, <booking link or two times>.

## After hours acknowledgement (sent next business morning)

Hi <First name>, thanks for your enquiry last night about <need>. <Owner name> will call you this morning before 10:00. If another time suits better, just reply.

## Missed call or voicemail

Hi <First name>, sorry I missed your call about <need>. I'll try you again at <time>, or call me back on <number> any time before 5pm.

## Out of area or out of scope

Hi <First name>,

Thanks for thinking of us. <Need> in <location> is outside what we do, so we're not the right fit this time. <If you know one: "You might try a local specialist; <referral type> usually handle this.">

All the best,
<Owner name>

## Rules

- Answer the question they asked in the first two lines.
- Offer one next step, not a menu.
- No prices unless the owner has approved standard pricing for this reply.
- No marketing content, newsletters or attachments they did not ask for.
- Match their tone; keep it under 120 words for email and 300 characters for SMS.
